# World
---
The Land Under is a twisted domain where the Gem Lords have altered the natural order. The dead rise as undead, slowly destroying the land. Crystalline forests, molten gem rivers, and gemstone cities are now haunted by these undead abominations.

# Main story
---
The Player is [[banished]] from their world and cast into the Land Under. The god of this desolate realm needs the Player to kill the Gem Lord and restore balance by becoming the new ruler of the Land Under. To achieve this, the Player must defeat five powerful bosses to gather the shattered gem shards needed to rebuild the new Gem Order of the Land Under. With the help of there [[Maiden]], a guide sent by the god, the Player will face numerous challenges. However, to create the Green Gem, Ava must sacrifice herself, enabling the Player to kill the ultimate Gem Lord and claim the Shader Gem to become the new lord.
# The History
---
The Land Under was once a peaceful land, where death and birth were in order, moon and stars moved in harmony, and night and day were linked. [[Cedric Blackthorn]], the first god who created this land, forged the gems of order with life and earth, maintaining the land's balance and prosperity.

One day, Cedric was betrayed and stabbed in the back by [[Gideon Stormcaller]], who sought to rule the Land Under. Gods cannot be killed, so Gideon imprisoned Cedric in the Green Gem, giving rise to the first Gem Lord. Gideon forged the gem with new rules, where the souls of the dead would end up under the Green Gem, granting him immortal power and life. This dark transformation corrupted the land, causing the rise of undead creatures and the distortion of natural order.

After the land fell into ruin, a banished soul rose for an adventure—one that would change this land once and for all. Banished and forged through hell, they strove to restore this land to the glorious place it once was, a place anyone would wish to call home. The Banished arrived at the Green Gem, hoping the Lord would choose them. As they touched the gem, their form slowly faded into it, and thus, [[Lord Bill]] became the new god of the Land Under.
