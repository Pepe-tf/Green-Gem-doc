# Green Gem

Welcome to **Green Gem**, a unique Minecraft server inspired by the captivating and challenging worlds of **Elden Ring** and the **Souls games**. Dive into an immersive world filled with tough enemies, strategic combat, rich lore, and a story that evolves based on your actions. Built from 2020 to 2025, Green Gem offers an unforgettable journey where your choices matter and every victory feels hard-earned.

---

## 🌟 Features

Green Gem offers a variety of exciting features to enhance your Minecraft experience:

- **Elden Ring / Souls Games-Inspired Gameplay**: Get ready for tough battles and unforgiving enemies. Only the most dedicated will survive!
- **Story-Driven Adventure**: Explore a deep narrative filled with mystery, lore, and unexpected twists.
- **Dynamic World**: The game world reacts to your actions, keeping every adventure fresh and impactful.
- **Custom Enemies & Bosses**: Encounter unique, high-difficulty bosses and enemies tailored to challenge even the most skilled players.
- **Questline & Events**: Participate in dynamic quests and world events that will uncover the secrets of Green Gem.
- **Minecraft Java Edition Server**: Built on Minecraft Java Edition, designed with custom mechanics to bring the Green Gem world to life.
- **AI Integration**: Powered by Ollama AI for advanced, reactive interactions to make your journey even more engaging.

---

## 🚀 Installation

To get started on your adventure in Green Gem, follow these simple installation steps:

1. **Download Minecraft Java Edition**: Make sure you have the latest version of **Minecraft Java Edition** installed. [Download here](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/download).
   
2. **Join the Green Gem Server**:
   - **Server IP**: `mc.ggem.buzz`
   - **Port**: `25565`

3. **Install Required Mods (if applicable)**: Some custom mods or resource packs may be required to fully experience all the features of Green Gem. Please check the [Green Gem website](https://green-gem.site) for any additional instructions.

4. **Connect and Play**: After setting up, simply connect to the server, and your adventure begins!

## ⚔️ Gameplay

Green Gem features a challenging and rewarding gameplay experience that will test your skills:

1. **Challenging Combat**: Combat in Green Gem is designed to be strategic and unforgiving. Every enemy you face presents a unique challenge that requires you to think carefully about your approach.
   
2. **Exploration**: Discover hidden secrets, ancient ruins, treasures, and lore scattered across the world. Every corner hides something valuable for those brave enough to explore.

3. **Progression**: As you venture deeper into the world, you’ll unlock powerful abilities, gear, and weapons to help you tackle increasingly difficult foes.

4. **Multiplayer**: Join forces with others or go solo to tackle tough bosses and explore the world. Whether you’re working together or fighting for your own survival, every player brings something unique to the table.
