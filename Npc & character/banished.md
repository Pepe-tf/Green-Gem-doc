# Player
----
The Player is banished from their world and cast into the Land Under. The god of this desolate realm needs the Player to kill the Gem Lord and restore balance by becoming the new ruler of the Land Under.

# World
---
The Land Under is a twisted domain where the Gem Lords have altered the natural order. The dead rise as undead, slowly destroying the land. Crystalline forests, molten gem rivers, and gemstone cities are now haunted by these undead abominations.
