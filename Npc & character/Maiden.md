# Ava
---
[[Ava]] is a lost Maiden who has forgotten her purpose in life and the land she once belonged to. Protected by the god, Lord Bill, she survives in anticipation of the arrival of the new Banished (player) in the Land Under. Lord Bill tasks Ava with guiding the Banished on their journey to become the Gem Lord while also helping her rediscover her own purpose along the way.
