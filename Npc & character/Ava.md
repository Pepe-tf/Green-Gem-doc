# Ava's Role
---
Ava is a guide sent by the god of the Land Under to assist the Player in their quest. She provides crucial knowledge and support as the Player faces numerous challenges in the twisted domain

# Ava's Sacrifice
----
With all five shards in hand, Ava reveals the tragic truth: to forge the Green Gem, the key to defeating the Gem Lord, she must sacrifice herself. This act is necessary to infuse the gem with the power needed to overcome the Gem Lord's might. Ava willingly gives her life, transforming the five shards into the powerful Green Gem.
